HYSA · high-yield savings account

A savings account that pays interest on cash you keep there. It can be useful for emergency money and goals you may need in the next few years.

What to compare

  • APY (the rate can change)
  • FDIC or NCUA insurance and coverage limits
  • Minimum balance and fees
  • Transfer timing, withdrawal rules and access
  • Promotional rates and eligibility requirements
Do not choose an account based only on the highest advertised APY. Confirm the institution, terms and fees directly before opening an account.

Roth IRA · retirement investing

A type of retirement account. You contribute eligible money you earned, then choose investments inside the account. Investment values can rise or fall; returns are not guaranteed.

Remember

  • You generally need eligible compensation from work to contribute.
  • Contributions are limited by IRS rules and your eligible earned income.
  • If you are under 18, a custodial Roth IRA may be needed, depending on the provider.
  • Opening an IRA does not automatically invest the money; you choose an investment.
  • Keep tuition and emergency money out of risky long-term investments.

Use it as a phone app

This is an installable website (Progressive Web App), not an App Store download. Once hosted on a secure HTTPS address, you can add it to your phone's home screen and open it like an app.

iPhone / iPad

Open the hosted planner in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Android

Open the hosted planner in Chrome → menu (⋮) → Install app or Add to Home screen.

If you opened the file directly from Downloads, it is not hosted yet and cannot be installed as a PWA. Upload the website folder to a static host with HTTPS first.
